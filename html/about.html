About

Rapper. Producer. Poet. Kampala, Uganda.

The Short Version

Maze the 28th is a rapper, producer, and poet from Kampala, Uganda, writing raw, introspective Hip-Hop that turns overthinking into self-discovery. His debut project "Into the Mind of an Overthinker" blends Hip-Hop, Soul, and Afro-centric influences into a sound that's unmistakably his own.

"Every track is a chapter. Every lyric is a truth."

The Full Story

Every bar Maze the 28th writes comes from someone who has actually lived through the overthinking, not just written about it from a distance. Raised in Kampala, he found in music a way to turn the restless, circling thoughts of everyday life into something that could be shared — verses that spiral through anxiety, relationships, and self-doubt until they land somewhere true.

That honesty is what shapes the sound as much as the lyrics. Blending Hip-Hop with Soul and Afro-centric elements, his music reflects an eclectic personality — raw and unfiltered in one verse, melodic and reflective in the next. It's a reflection of the journey itself: overthinking as the starting point, self-discovery as the destination.

Today, that journey continues through mixtapes, live shows, and a debut album built chapter by chapter. Every release is another page in the same story — one about staying true to yourself even when the mind won't stay quiet.
