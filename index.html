Into the Mind of an Overthinker

Maze the 28th writes the way a mind actually moves — restless, honest, circling back until a thought becomes a truth. Every bar comes from someone who has lived through the overthinking, not just written about it.

Blending Hip-Hop, Soul, and Afro-centric influences out of Kampala, Uganda, his music turns self-doubt into self-discovery — raw lyrics about adversity, relationships, and staying true to yourself.

"Every track is a chapter. Every lyric is a truth."
