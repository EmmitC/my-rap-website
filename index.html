About

Emmit Christopher Ebau, aka Maze 28, a multi-talented artist,rapper,singer, songwriter, producer, and poet,born in Kampala, Uganda on August 10th, 2002.

Standing at 5ft11 with dark brown eyes and black hair, I'm a creative force to be reckoned with.

My music is a reflection of my experiences, thoughts, and emotions, all woven together with a unique blend of lyrics and melodies.

With a passion for music and poetry, honed and refined his skills and voice.

Known for his story telling prowess,thought provoking lyrics,satire and social commentary, on a mission to share my art with the world, inspiring and connecting with like-minded individuals along the way.
